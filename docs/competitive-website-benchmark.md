## Patterns to borrow, not copy
- Tie AI to business goals, operational needs and measurable value rather than selling AI as a novelty.
- Show a journey from opportunity discovery to prioritisation, implementation and measurement.
- Make deliverables explicit: assessment, opportunity map, roadmap, pilot, implementation and follow-up.
- Explain risk, adoption and prerequisites; avoid guaranteed ROI claims.
- Keep the first step low-friction and concrete.

## Cidea decisions
Retain the deep-navy strategy identity with a more ownable teal/mint accent. Turn thin service stubs into substantive pages that explain who each service is for, what the client receives, how it works and what to do next.
