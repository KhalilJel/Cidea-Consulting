# Market-inspired website refresh — Cidea Consulting

## Benchmark-derived decisions
- Lead with business value and real workflows, not AI hype or tool lists.
- Make the customer journey explicit: identify opportunity, prioritise, implement and improve.
- Use practical examples and explain what the customer receives.
- Keep proof honest: no invented case studies, metrics or testimonials.

## Visual direction
- Keep the deep-navy consulting foundation, distinct from Marketing and Leads.
- Shift the main accent from conventional bright blue to an ownable teal/mint.
- Retain restrained warm amber as a secondary strategic accent where already used.
- Improve mobile navigation so every primary service remains reachable.

## Implementation sequence
1. Validate service routes, language behaviour, CTA links and mobile layouts.
2. Apply the teal/mint accent consistently to maps, focus states, labels and buttons.
3. Make the service model and implementation steps easy to scan.
4. Review service pages, commercial offer and proof.
5. Verify keyboard focus, reduced motion, responsive widths and build.

## Guardrails
No production deployment, DNS/MX changes, prospect emails, fabricated client results or secrets in Git.
