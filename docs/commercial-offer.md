# Commercial offer — Cidea Consulting

## Positioning
Cidea Consulting connects AI opportunities to measurable business value.

## Entry point
**Uforpliktende vurdering** — no cost, no obligation. We identify areas where AI may save time, reduce cost or create capacity.

## Paid offers
### AI Opportunity Report — 7 500 NOK
A structured assessment of selected workflows, opportunities, expected impact, complexity and recommended next steps.

### AI Business Workshop — 12 500 NOK
A practical workshop for leadership or teams to identify and prioritize AI use cases in their own business.

### AI Implementation — from 25 000 NOK
Build and integrate a validated AI workflow or internal system after the opportunity has been assessed.

### AI Agent Systems — scoped
Custom agent systems are scoped after the workflow, tools, data and risk requirements are understood.

## Sales flow
Assessment → opportunity report or workshop → implementation when justified.

## Rule
Never recommend AI because it is fashionable. If a simpler non-AI solution is better, recommend that instead.
