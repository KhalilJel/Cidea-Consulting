# How do you calculate the ROI of AI?

AI should be treated as a business project, not only a technology project. Before you invest, you should understand which problem will be solved and what value the solution can create.

## Start with the current situation

Map how much time, money or capacity the process uses today. Also look at errors, waiting time and manual work.

## Estimate the potential gain

A simple assessment can compare the expected time saved, reduced cost or increased capacity with the cost of building and running the solution.

## Include complexity and risk

A solution with a high theoretical gain isn't necessarily a good first project. Data quality, integrations, security, human oversight and changes to ways of working all affect the real value.

## Prioritise the best opportunities

Start with use cases where the problem is clear, the value can be measured and delivery is realistic. The business can then build on what actually works.
