# Where can AI create value in a business?

AI doesn't create value just because a company adopts a new tool. Value appears when the technology solves a real problem or improves a work process.

## Look for friction

Good candidates are often tasks that are repeated frequently, require a lot of manual handling, involve large amounts of information or have clear bottlenecks.

Examples include research, document handling, internal knowledge, customer dialogue, reporting and parts of the sales process.

## Prioritise before you build

A good AI assessment looks at potential value, complexity, risk and how well the solution fits the business.

The goal isn't to automate as much as possible. The goal is to find what's worth automating.
