# How do you find good AI use cases?

The best way to find AI opportunities is to start with the business, not the technology.

## Look for friction

Begin with tasks that are repeated often, take a lot of time, require manual handling or create bottlenecks. That could be analysis, document handling, customer dialogue, research, reporting or internal processes.

## Assess four things

For each possible use of AI, ask:

1. How much time or cost could be affected?
2. How often does the process happen?
3. How difficult is it to implement?
4. What risk arises if the solution makes a mistake?

A simple matrix of value, complexity and risk makes it easier to prioritise.

## Don't automate just because you can

An AI solution is only interesting if it solves a real problem better than the current way of working. Some processes should still be handled manually.

That's exactly why a good AI assessment should come before implementation.
